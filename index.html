On your watch

    Getting the workout onto a Garmin watch
    1. Connect the watch to a computer with its USB cable. On a Mac, recent watches need a file transfer app such as OpenMTP.
    2. Copy the .fit file into the GARMIN/NewFiles folder, then unplug the watch.
    3. Find it in the pool swim activity under Training > Workouts. Menu names vary by model.

    Garmin Connect does not import workout FIT files, so this has to be done over USB. Apple Watch does not read FIT files.

    How the workout behaves in the pool
    • Kick and drill steps: the watch cannot count lengths without arm strokes, so expect to press lap to finish them.
    • Zones: shown as text with their RPE, not as heart-rate alerts, because wrist heart rate is unreliable in water.
    • Rest ranges and send-offs (R1-2min, on 3:00): the watch waits for the lap button.
    Notation it understands
    • 5x200 R20 @Z3: reps, distance, rest in seconds, zone. Also R1:30, R2min, R1-2min, on 3:00.
    • 3x on its own line repeats the lines below it, up to the next blank line. - Set 1: … changes one round.
    • 10x200 R60 as with - 2x @Z3 lines breaks the reps down. Odds: and Evens: work too.
    • (50 MAX + 150 easy), [25 drill / 75 swim]: parts of one rep, with no rest between them.
    • drill/swim: half of each. fins, pull, paddles, snorkel, board: equipment.
    • Warm up, Main set, Warm down: sections. - 2.9km: the stated total, checked against the sets.
